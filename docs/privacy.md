# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Brain is built to keep your memories on your own device.

## What Brain stores

Everything you add (text, links, screenshots, PDFs, notes, tags, types and the
details you type in) is stored in a database on your device. Brain has no
accounts and no Brain servers. The developer cannot see your memories.

## What leaves your device

- **Links you save.** To read a web page you saved, Brain downloads that page
  directly from its website. That website sees an ordinary request from your
  device, as if you had opened the page.
- **Update checks (desktop).** The desktop app checks this repository on GitHub
  for new versions. GitHub sees the request as described in
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Brain does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and
it does not sell or share your data.

## Your control

You can export your memories, move them to the Bin, or delete them. Uninstalling
Brain and removing its data folder deletes everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
