# What's new

Releases are listed newest first. Each platform has its own version tag
(`desktop-v…`, `android-v…`, `ios-v…`).

## Desktop 0.1.0 (7 October 2026)

The first desktop release of Brain, for Windows and macOS.

- **Save anything you want to remember:** text, links, screenshots and PDFs.
  Brain reads links and PDFs in the background and keeps the evidence it found.
- **Types and details:** choose a type (book, film, song, place, recipe and more)
  and fill in details that suit it, like singer and movie for a song, or
  destination type and trip style for a place. Add your own fields too.
- **Organize:** custom types up to three levels, tags, notes, an Inbox for
  memories that need a look, and a Bin you can restore from.
- **Find things again:** search your library or ask the Archivist, which only
  looks through what you saved.
- **Private by design:** everything stays on your computer. No account, no
  analytics.
- **Updates:** the Windows app checks for new versions on its own. On macOS,
  download new versions from the releases page for now.

Known limits: screenshots are kept but not read yet (no text recognition), and
the vault isn't encrypted yet. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning and macOS
asks you to choose **Open Anyway** on first launch, until the apps are signed.
